# NeuraAPI — Break-up email (Day 12)

**Cadence:** Send 12 days after Email 1 (5 days after Follow-up 2) if no reply.
**Goal:** Permission-based close. Give them an easy out, leave the door open, offer one last free resource. Often gets the highest reply rate of the sequence.
**Tone:** Respectful, short, no guilt.

---

## Variant A — Freelance developer

**Subject:** `closing your file, {{first_name}}?`

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

I'll take the hint — no reply means this isn't a priority right now, and that's completely fine.

I'm closing your file so I stop cluttering your inbox. Two things I'm leaving you with, no strings:

- Free starter (auth + dashboard + SEO): https://ai-empire-steel.vercel.app/free
- Template catalog (launch `LANCEMENT30` = -30%): https://ai-empire-steel.vercel.app/templates

If a future client project needs a SaaS/e-commerce/blog base, just reply to this thread — I'll pick it back up.

Cheers,
Sam
NeuraAPI

**CTA:** None (open door). Optional: "Reply 'later' and I'll ping you in 3 months."

## Sending notes
- This email often gets the highest reply rate — keep it genuinely permission-based.
- After sending: mark prospect as "closed-lost" in `~/porte-a-frapper/PORTES.md`, set a 90-day re-touch reminder.
- If they reply "later"/"q3"/"ship", move to a nurture tag and do NOT send more of this sequence.
- Never send a 5th email. Respect the break-up.
